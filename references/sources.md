# Sources and further reading

This skill is a distillation, written in its own words, of ideas from the works below. Nothing here reproduces their text; read the originals for depth and examples.

## Primary influences on the visual rules

- Adam Wathan & Steve Schoger — *Refactoring UI* (hierarchy by de-emphasis, spacing systems, type scale, HSL ramps, depth with one light source, finishing touches)
- Adham Dannaway — *Practical UI* (1st & 2nd ed.) (token-based UI systems, accessible color, spacing tokens, component consistency)
- Michał & Diana Malewicz — *Designing User Interfaces*
- Jenifer Tidwell et al. — *Designing Interfaces* (interaction patterns)
- Josef Müller-Brockmann — *Grid Systems in Graphic Design*
- Timothy Samara — *Design Elements: A Graphic Style Manual*; *Design Evolution*
- Stefan G. Bucher — *Graphic Design Rules*
- UXPin — *Web UI Design for the Human Eye*
- Diana MacDonald — *Practical UI Patterns for Design Systems*
- Marco Suarez, Jina Anne et al. — *Design Systems Handbook*

## Color

- Sean Adams — *The Designer's Dictionary of Color*
- Steven Bleicher — *Contemporary Color Theory and Use*
- Richard Mehl — *Playing with Color*

## Measurement framework

- Pratyush Tewari — "UX Principles for Modern UI/UX Design and Their Measurement: A Framework for Digital Product Excellence", *Journal of Computer Science and Technology Studies* 7(7), 2025. DOI 10.32996/jcsts.2025.7.7.99 (CC-BY 4.0). Source of the Clear / Efficient / Smart / Connected / Polished lens (the Terra design system) and the CES / SUS / usability-testing / behavioral-metrics measurement set used in `review-rubric.md` and `ux-psychology.md`.

## Skills and repos consulted

- ceorkm/mobile-app-ui-design (MIT) — mobile process, thumb-zone and state rules, peak–end application; its decoration advice (glow, glass, `rounded-3xl`) is deliberately not followed here.
- Manuelvillarvieites/n8n-and-claude skills — `butter-smooth-scrolling`, `typography-audit`, `colors-typography`, `section-backgrounds`, `framer-animations`, `page-transitions`, `button-unification` — audit-style checks and motion timing values; scroll hijacking and curtain transitions treated as opt-in, not default.
- ytx-readings/design-ui-ux — the book collection above.
